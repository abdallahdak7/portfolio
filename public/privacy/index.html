Privacy notice

Last updated 28 October 2026

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. Here is what it and Tom, its assistant, collect, why, who processes it and for how long.

Who is responsible

Abdallah Daknache, Limassol, Cyprus, is responsible for this site and the data it handles. For any question or request about your data, email abdallah.daknache@gmail.com.

Audience figures

To see how many people visit and what they find useful, the site uses Umami Cloud, a cookieless analytics service. It records each page view with the referring site, your country, device type and browser, and three events: opening the Résumé, Tom first answering you, and following a link to contact Abdallah (booking a call, email or LinkedIn) or sending him a Message through Tom.

No cookies or identifiers are stored on your device, and your IP address is not stored. The figures are counts about visitors as a group; they can't single you out, and nothing you write to Tom is ever included. The basis is Abdallah's legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used.

Ask Tom

When you chat with Tom:

  • Your Conversation is kept only in your browser and sent with each question so Tom has context. Tom's service does not store it.
  • To keep the service fair and affordable, it counts questions per network address and per Conversation. These counts expire within 24 hours.
  • Tom's answers are generated by Anthropic's AI model, which receives your Conversation to write each reply. The service runs on Cloudflare. Its error logs never contain what you wrote.

Messages

If you ask Tom to pass a Message to Abdallah, your name, email address and text are emailed to him through Cloudflare's email service. He uses them only to reply to you, and deletes them when they're no longer needed for that. The basis is your request.

Human check

Before Tom answers, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're a person, to keep automated abuse away from Tom. Cloudflare uses what it collects only for that check, not for advertising.

Dictation

If you speak a question instead of typing it, your browser turns your speech into text. Some browsers send the audio to their vendor's speech service (for example Google or Apple) to do this. This site never receives your audio, only the text you choose to send. You can always type instead.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security. Fonts are served from this site, not a third party.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete data about you, or object to its use, by emailing the address above. You can also complain to Cyprus's Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.
